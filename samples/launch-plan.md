# Launch Plan — Ajaia Docs Beta

A short plan for opening the beta to the first **three partner teams**. Everything here is *draft* until the review on Friday.

## Goals

- Ship the editor with **autosave** and *conflict detection*
- Let owners share a document with a named teammate
- Import existing notes from `.md`, `.txt` and `.docx`

## Timeline

1. **Week 1** — internal dogfooding with the docs team
2. **Week 2** — invite partner teams, collect feedback
3. **Week 3** — fix the top five issues and publish release notes

### Risks

- Imports may drop *tables and images* — this is expected and documented
- Two people editing at once will see a **conflict banner** instead of a merge
